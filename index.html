A tiny deterministic world, shown in human terms. Step through it, run forty simulated years, and watch luck, talent, and inequality pull the same starting population apart.

Reference world · Talent × Luck

1,000 people start equally wealthy. Then chance gets a vote.

Everyone begins with wealth 10. Talent is fixed at birth. Each half-year, lucky opportunities can double wealth and misfortunes can halve it. The world runs for 80 steps, or 40 simulated years.

  • TTalent is distributed around 0.6 and does not change during the run.
  • +Lucky opportunity has a 10% × talent chance per person per step and doubles wealth.
  • −Misfortune has a 10% chance per person per step and halves wealth.

What should I notice?

Watch inequality and the wealth spread grow even though everyone starts at the same wealth.

Then compare that with the talent ↔ wealth correlation. A positive correlation can coexist with a large role for luck.

Same world + same seed = same result. Change the seed to explore another possible history, then return to 42 to replay it exactly.
